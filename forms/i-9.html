What is Form I-9?

Form I-9 is used to verify the identity and employment authorization of every person hired for employment in the United States, whether they are a citizen or not.

Who fills it in, and where does it go?

The new employee completes Section 1 and the employer completes Section 2. The employer keeps the completed form — it is not filed with USCIS or ICE, but must be available for inspection by authorized government officials.

How to fill it here

Click the boxes in Section 1 and type your details, or, as an employer, complete Section 2. Download the filled PDF when you are done and add the required signatures.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving required Section 1 boxes empty.
  • Using an outdated edition of the form.
  • Sending the form to USCIS — it is kept by the employer.

Frequently asked questions

Do I send Form I-9 to USCIS?

No. USCIS states that Form I-9 must not be filed with USCIS or ICE. The employer keeps it.

Which edition is this?

This is the edition dated 01/20/25, downloaded from USCIS.
