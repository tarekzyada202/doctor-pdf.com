What does converting images to PDF mean?

Converting images to PDF means turning one or more pictures — photos, scans, or screenshots — into a single PDF document, with each image becoming a page. A PDF is far easier to send, print, and archive than a folder of loose image files, and it keeps your pages in a fixed order that looks the same on every device.

With Doctor PDF you add your images, arrange their order, and download one clean PDF — perfect for turning phone photos of documents into a proper file.

When do you need to convert images to PDF?

  • Turning phone photos of a contract or ID into one PDF to submit.
  • Combining several scanned pages into a single document.
  • Creating a PDF portfolio or catalog from images.
  • Sending screenshots as one tidy file instead of many images.
  • Preparing image-based documents for a form that requires PDF.

How Doctor PDF builds your PDF privately

Your images are turned into a PDF entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, so photos of personal documents never leave your device or touch a server.

Because it all runs locally, conversion is instant and there is no limit on the number of images or the file size.

How to convert images to PDF online
