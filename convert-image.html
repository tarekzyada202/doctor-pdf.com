What does converting an image mean?

Converting an image means changing it from one format to another — for example turning a PNG into a JPG, a HEIC photo into a standard JPG, or any image into modern WebP. Each format has a purpose: JPG for small photos, PNG for transparency and sharp graphics, WebP for the smallest size on the web. Converting lets you meet a website's required format, shrink a file, or make an image open on any device.

With Doctor PDF you drop an image, pick the output format, optionally resize it, and download the converted file instantly — with no quality lost to a server round-trip.

When do you need to convert an image?

  • A form or website only accepts JPG but your image is PNG or HEIC.
  • Shrinking a large photo by converting it to WebP or JPG.
  • Turning a screenshot into a smaller, shareable format.
  • Converting a transparent logo to PNG, or a photo to JPG for printing.
  • Making an iPhone HEIC photo open on Windows or older devices.
